About Shree Laxmi Vilas

Shree Laxmi Vilas Family Restaurant is a family-friendly Indian restaurant and bar in Malad East, Mumbai. Our kitchen is known for flavourful biryanis and a wide range of starters, from paneer and mushroom to chicken and egg.

Whether it is a family dinner, a group meal in the private dining room or a late-night craving, we aim to serve food that feels like home. We are rated 4.7 on Google.
